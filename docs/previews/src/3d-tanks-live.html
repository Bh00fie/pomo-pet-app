Spike · branch explore/3d-aquarium

Three tank shapes, one containment function

These are running the spike's real swim math and real fish geometry — the port lives in docs/previews/src/tank-renderer.js, drawn to a 2D canvas so the page stays a few kilobytes instead of shipping three.js. On device the same scene runs through expo-gl with proper lighting and depth. What this shows faithfully is the shape language and the motion; what it does not show is material quality or frame cost.

Fish count

The report's numbers are quoted at 20 — 1,760 triangles, ~80 draw calls.

Why this matters for the IAP

Adding a tank SKU is adding a record to src/features/aquarium3d/tanks.ts plus one containment function — a box clamps per axis, a cylinder clamps the horizontal radius, a bowl normalises onto a sphere. Swim behaviour is authored once against a unit volume and never changes. Because containment is the shape equation rather than a per-frame collision test, fish cannot clip through the glass no matter what shape you sell next.

The same pattern works in 2D Skia with a Path and a clip. So tank shapes are not a reason to adopt 3D — they are only nicer in 3D.
