Decision · branch explore/3d-aquarium · 2 Aug 2026

Is a 3D aquarium worth it for v1?

Defer to v2

Ship v1 on 2D Skia. Keep the branch.

Not because 3D fails. The two risks most likely to kill it both came back clean: it does not break the free Expo Go plan — expo-gl is a bundled Expo Go module on SDK 54, so M0–M6a survive intact — and it builds today on the exact pinned stack, with a working three-shape prototype to prove it.

The reason to defer is the per-species art cost, which is precisely where this app makes its money. In 2D a new species is a parameter record you author in an afternoon. In 3D it is a modelled, unwrapped, textured, rigged asset — days of work or real money, per SKU, forever. Adopting 3D converts the cheapest revenue lever into the most expensive one.

Measured

Estimated

Every frame-rate, battery and thermal figure. Nothing was run on a physical iPhone — this machine has Command Line Tools only, and Three.js + EXGL is documented as unreliable on simulators anyway. Ten minutes with Expo Go on a real phone converts these to facts.

What would change the answer

If the aquarium turns out to be the product rather than the reward — if you would rather sell a few beautiful tanks than many cheap species — the art-cost argument inverts and 3D becomes right. That is a product call, not an engineering one. Revisit it at the M6a decision gate, when you have used the free build daily and know whether the tank is the thing you actually open the app for.
